snapPDF Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026
Last updated: September 6, 2026

Thank you for using snapPDF (the "App"). The App is developed by kikiwen ("we", "us", or "our") to scan, organize invoice photos, and generate PDF files.

We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information related to you.

1. Overview

The App is a local-only utility. We do not collect, upload, or store your personal data on any server. Invoice photos you capture or import, and PDFs you generate, are stored only in the App's private storage on your device.

The App:

  • Does not require registration or sign-in
  • Does not use advertising or third-party analytics
  • Does not send your data to our servers or any third party

2. Information We Process

2.1 Content You Provide

When you use the App, the following data is processed and stored locally on your device:

Data typePurposeStorage
Photos taken with the cameraScan and save invoicesApp private directory on device
Photos selected from your galleryImport existing invoice imagesApp private directory on device
Generated PDF filesOrganize, preview, and shareApp private directory on device

These files may contain business information visible on invoices (merchant name, amount, date, etc.). You provide this content yourself; we do not access or back it up in the cloud.

2.2 Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions, used only after you grant them:

PermissionPurpose
CameraCapture invoice photos and use document scanning
Photo libraryImport invoice images from your gallery

You may revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. Revoking a permission disables the related feature but does not delete data already stored in the App.

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect:

  • Account information such as name, email, or phone number
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reporting data
  • Contacts, microphone, or other data unrelated to App functionality

3. How We Use Data

We use the data described above only on your device to:

  • Display and manage your invoice photos
  • Merge, compress, and generate PDFs from selected photos
  • Share PDF files via the system share sheet to apps you choose (email, cloud storage, messaging, etc.)

Sharing is entirely initiated by you. When you use the system share feature, files are passed to the third-party app you select. That app's data practices are governed by its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All photos and PDFs are stored in the App's private document directory; other apps cannot access them by default.
  • On iOS, if Files app access is enabled, you may browse PDFs in the App through the system file manager.
  • The App does not perform cloud backup. Back up important files yourself via the share feature.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all photos and PDFs in its private directory with no recovery. Back up important files before uninstalling.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or social SDKs. It relies on system-level capabilities that run on your device and do not transmit your content to the developer:

  • System camera and photo library (provided by iOS / Android)
  • System share sheet (provided by iOS / Android)
  • Local file system (accessed via Expo / React Native on your device)

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us using the details below.

7. Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • View all photos and PDFs in the App
  • Delete individual or multiple photos and PDF files
  • Export PDFs to other apps via the system share feature
  • Erase everything by uninstalling the App

If you are in the EU (GDPR), California (CCPA), or another region with statutory data rights, we do not collect or process identifiable personal data on our servers; exercising those rights is primarily done through the local controls above.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. For material changes, we will notify you in the App or by other appropriate means. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.
